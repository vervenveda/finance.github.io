Financial position

Net savings after debt includes only entered savings and outstanding debt; it excludes home equity, retirement investments, other assets, and liabilities not recorded here. Debt balance / annual income is not the lender debt-payment-to-income (DTI) ratio. A full DTI requires monthly debt payments and gross monthly income.

Debt Freedom Studio

Compare snowball (smallest balance first) and avalanche (highest APR first). Estimates assume fixed monthly interest, no new purchases or fees, and minimum payments that remain constant. All calculations run locally; scenarios are not saved.

Goal completion estimator

Data sovereignty and recovery

Your data is stored only in this browser's localStorage. Clearing browser data may erase it. Export regularly to a location you control. Imports are validated and require confirmation before replacing current command-center data.
